consulting

For the last 5+ years I’ve been working with the independent think tank Demos Helsinki on a range of consulting projects supporting institutional change in public governance. While working across domains as diverse as sustainable development and social welfare, my main area of expertise is innovation policy.

Previous collaborations include governments and public agencies (Finland, Sweden, UK, European Commission), philanthropies (Laudes, Bloomberg), private companies (Meta) and NGOs (Housing First Europe Hub).

Types of projects include:

  • keynotes
  • tailored training programmes
  • policy analysis and evaluation
  • systems mapping reports
  • interactive workshops
  • narrative pieces
  • white papers
