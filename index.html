hello!

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy at UCL IIPP. My research focuses on the role of public management in policy implementation. With this work, I aim to explore plausible avenues for renewing state capacity in contemporary European liberal democracy.

My doctoral thesis investigates the implementation of European net-zero mission-oriented innovation policy and the institutional outcomes it achieved through the last decade (2015-2025). Theoretically, it employs an interdisciplinary approach bridging public administration and political economy research. Methodologically, it combines Qualitative Comparative Analysis and process tracing to analyse survey, interview, and ethnographic data. During this research, I have been affiliated as a visiting researcher at the:

  • Ministry of Climate and Green Growth (NL)
  • General Secretariat for Investment (FR)
  • National Innovation Agency (UK)
  • National Innovation Fund (DK)

Besides, I work on a range of consulting projects as a senior expert at independent think tank Demos Helsinki and occasionally serve as a guest lecturer at UCL IIPP. I hold two MA degrees in Political Science from the Sant’Anna School of Advanced Studies and the University of Florence. Previously, I worked as a research assistant at the Italian Institute for Public Policy Analysis (INAPP) and as an MP assistant at the Italian Chamber of Deputies.
